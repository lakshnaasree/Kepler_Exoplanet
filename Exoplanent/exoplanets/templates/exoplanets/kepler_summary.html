ABOUT KEPLER

The Planet Hunter

NASA's Kepler Space Telescope was designed to search for exoplanets by monitoring the brightness of stars over time. When a planet passes in front of its star, it can cause a small decrease in the observed brightness.

MISSION TIMELINE

From Launch to Legacy

2009

Mission Launch

NASA launched the Kepler Space Telescope to search for planets beyond our Solar System.

2013

K2 Mission

After the original mission faced spacecraft pointing limitations, Kepler continued its scientific work through the K2 mission.

2014

Extended Exploration

The K2 mission expanded Kepler's observations to additional regions of the sky.

2018

Mission End

NASA retired the Kepler spacecraft after completing its planet-hunting mission.

WHAT KEPLER REVEALED

Discoveries That Changed Exoplanet Science

01

Planets Are Common

Kepler observations showed that planets are widespread throughout our galaxy.

02

Diverse Planetary Systems

The mission revealed a wide variety of planetary systems and orbital configurations.

03

Small Planets Exist

Kepler helped identify many planets that are much smaller than the gas giants of our Solar System.

04

New Worlds to Explore

Kepler dramatically expanded the catalog of known planets beyond our Solar System.
